# Mexico investing facts (verified 2026-09-21)

Primary sources: LISR (DOF 2024-04-01), SAT Anexo 7 RMF 2026 (DOF 2026-01-09),
GBM fee and product pages, 26 CFR 20.2104-1, IRS non-resident estate guidance.
Re-verify yearly; GBM terms change without notice.

## Capital gains (persona física)

- **The SIC listing of the security decides the 10% rate, not the broker.** Gains
  on foreign shares listed in the SIC (BMV or BIVA) are 10% definitive under
  LISR Art. 129 fr. I, including when bought and sold through a foreign broker
  such as IBKR or GBM Trading USA (SAT criterio normativo 37/ISR/N).
- Securities **not** listed in the SIC and sold abroad are Title IV Chapter IV
  income (Arts. 119–124) at progressive rates.
- **Contested:** SIC-listed equity ETFs through a foreign broker (criterio 37
  names shares; the ETF route relies on fr. II). Non-equity ETFs (bonds,
  commodities) through a foreign broker are doubtful.
- Through a Mexican broker the intermediary issues the constancia/CFDI. Through a
  foreign broker there is no withholding and no constancia: the person computes
  the MXN gain (average cost, INPC update) and declares it in April.

## Dividends

- US dividends: 10% US withholding with a W-8BEN (30% without). Mexico adds them
  to annual income with an Art. 5 credit and applies the additional definitive
  10% (Art. 142 fr. V). Through the SIC the intermediary withholds it; through a
  foreign broker it is due by the 17th of the following month.
- Irish accumulating UCITS (e.g. CSPX, listed in the SIC as CSPXN) distribute
  nothing, so there is no dividend event until sale.

## US estate tax

- US-domiciled stocks and ETFs are US-situs for a non-US person wherever they are
  custodied, including through the SIC. Exemption US$60k, rates up to 40%, no
  US–Mexico estate treaty.
- Irish UCITS are not US-situs. For a Mexican resident buying S&P 500 exposure,
  CSPX avoids this exposure; IVV/VOO do not.

## GBM

- **Trading MX (BMV and SIC):** whole shares only. Commission 0.25% (≤ MXN 1M
  invested over 3 months), tiering down to 0.10% above MXN 10M, **plus 16% IVA**.
  SIC prices are the home price × FX; place limit orders near that value.
- **Trading USA (DriveWealth, NYSE/Nasdaq):** fractional shares from US$1, 0.25%
  per trade, funded in USD from Smart Cash, FX spread not published. No CFDI;
  SIC-listed tickers still get 10%, others are progressive.
- **Smart Cash:** no commission. There is no zero-commission equity plan.

## Rules for the assistant

1. Before saying "accumulate cash for a whole share", check the MXN price; most
   SIC tickers cost far less than MXN 10k, and Trading USA offers fractions.
2. For a Mexican resident buying US exposure, always weigh: SIC listing (10% vs
   progressive), US-situs estate exposure (US-domiciled vs Irish UCITS),
   dividend treatment (distributing vs accumulating), commission + IVA, SIC
   premium and whole-share lot size.
3. Say "contested" for SIC-listed ETFs through a foreign broker.
